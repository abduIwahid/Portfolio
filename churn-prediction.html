Customer Churn Prediction

A machine-learning workflow for identifying churn risk and evaluating classification models responsibly.

Problem

Customer churn models estimate which customers may leave so a business can investigate risk and plan retention efforts.

Workflow

  • Inspect and clean customer and subscription data.
  • Prepare features and define a suitable train/test evaluation strategy.
  • Compare classification models against appropriate baselines.
  • Evaluate using metrics beyond accuracy where class imbalance makes that necessary.
  • Translate model outputs into understandable risk indicators.

What to report

When publishing results, include dataset provenance, leakage checks, class balance, cross-validation or holdout strategy, and metrics such as precision, recall, F1, and ROC-AUC where appropriate. Do not report metrics unless they are reproduced from the actual experiment.
